Why Live or Invest in a Palm Hills Project?

A Trusted Delivery Record

Over 38 delivered projects and 13 currently under construction, and over 70,000 families actually living in them — a delivery commitment that's more than promises on paper.

70% Green Spaces

Palm Hills dedicates 70% of its project land to green spaces and natural landscaping — a low building density means more privacy and real quality of life.

Fully-Integrated, Self-Contained Communities

Not just residential units — schools and universities, sports clubs, malls, and medical centers inside every compound, letting you live your whole life without needing to leave.

Flexible Payment Plans

Down payments starting from 1.5% and installments up to 12 years — plans designed to fit both residents and investors, without pressuring the budget.

Spread Across Prime Locations

Projects in New Cairo and October, the North Coast and Ras Al Hekma, and the New Capital — you can choose between year-round living, a summer home, or both.

Investment Value That Keeps Growing

Sustained demand for resale and rental across all of the company's projects, with sales exceeding EGP 72 billion — a sign of an asset that holds its value and keeps growing over time.

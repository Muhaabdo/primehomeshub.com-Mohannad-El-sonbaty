Palm Hills — Badya

Why Choose Badya?

Badya isn't just a compound — it's a smart, fully-integrated green city in the heart of October Gardens, designed to give you a different way of life from day one.

A Smart, Fully-Integrated Green City

Badya sits on 3,000 feddans, over 50% of it green spaces and waterways, built on a "smart sustainable city" concept powered by solar energy and AI-driven home and security systems.

Strategic Location

Minutes from 6th of October City, and close to Mall of Egypt and the Pyramids.

Integrated Education, Healthcare & Retail

4 international schools and an international university, a hospital with the latest equipment and specialized emergency clinics, and 6 retail centers with global brands — everything you need inside the city.

West Cairo's Largest Sports Facilities

18 sports clubs, including the largest sports club in West Cairo on 85 feddans, horse-riding stables supervised by an international trainer, running and cycling tracks, and multi-depth swimming pools.

A Huge Variety of Units Across 15 Phases

Over 30,000 residential units spread across 15 phases (such as Dye, Zoy, District 6, Tali, and Moon Heights), including standalone villas, twin houses, townhouses, apartments, penthouses, and studios.

Flexible Payment Plans, Trusted Developer

Down payment from 1.5% and installments up to 12 years, from Palm Hills Developments — over 25 years of experience and 9 international awards.

Because you're investing in a fully-integrated city with a world-class concept, over 50% green and water spaces, and a trusted developer that secures continuous growth in property value.

1. Introduction

Prime Homes Hub ("the Platform", "we", "us", or "our"), based in the Arab Republic of Egypt and available at primehomeshub.com, respects the privacy of its visitors and users and is committed to protecting their personal data in accordance with this policy, Egyptian Law No. 151 of 2020 on the Protection of Personal Data, and Google's policies on advertising and personal data.

By using this website or submitting any contact form on it, you agree to the collection and use of your data as described in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the website or submit your data.

2. Data We Collect

When you interact with the website, particularly by filling out contact forms to inquire about our real estate projects, we may collect:

  • Full name.
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number.
  • Email address (if included in the form).
  • Details of your inquiry or the real estate project you are interested in.
  • Non-personal technical data such as browser type, device used, and referring page, collected automatically through analytics and Google Ads tools.

We do not collect payment or bank card data through this website, nor do we request any sensitive data unrelated to the purpose of real estate contact.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • Contacting you to respond to your inquiry about Hacienda Ras Al Hekma or any other project we feature.
  • Providing suitable real estate consultation through our consultant.
  • Sending additional information about a project, such as pricing, payment plans, and location, upon your request.
  • Improving website content and user experience, and measuring the effectiveness of our Google Ads campaigns.
  • Complying with any legal or regulatory requirements applicable in the Arab Republic of Egypt.

We do not use your data for any third-party marketing purpose unrelated to our real estate business without your explicit consent.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on your explicit consent given when submitting a contact form on the website, and on our legitimate interest in responding to your inquiry about our real estate services. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us using the details below.

5. Sharing Data With Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. We may share limited data only in the following cases:

  • Real estate developers: If you inquire about a specific project, we may share your name and phone number with the developer or its sales team responsible for that project, solely to facilitate contact and consultation.
  • Technical service providers: Such as website hosting services and messaging tools (e.g. WhatsApp Business), strictly within the scope of providing the service.
  • Advertising and analytics platforms: Such as Google Ads and Google Analytics, for measuring campaign performance and improving targeting, in accordance with their own privacy policies.
  • Legal authorities: If required by law or a court order issued by a competent authority in Egypt.

6. Cookies & Google Ads

This website uses cookies and similar technologies from Google Ads and Google Analytics for the purposes of:

  • Measuring the performance of our Google Search Ads campaigns.
  • Conversion tracking to understand how many inquiries result from our advertising.
  • Showing you tailored ads on other websites based on your visit to this site (remarketing), where enabled.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and you can visit Google Ads Settings to manage the personalized ads shown to you.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected — contacting you and providing the requested real estate consultation — unless a longer retention period is required under Egyptian law. Afterward, your data is securely deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Rights Over Your Data

Under the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data from our records.
  • Withdraw your consent to the use of your data at any time.
  • Object to the use of your data for certain marketing purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly using the details at the end of this page.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, and we continually work to improve our safeguards.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking information about real estate and investment opportunities, and we do not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal or operational changes. Any update will be posted on this page along with a revised "Last updated" date above, and we encourage you to review this page periodically.

Location & Connectivity

In the Heart of Ras Al Hekma, on the Alexandria - Marsa Matrouh Road

Hacienda Ras Al Hekma sits at kilometer 238 of the Alexandria – Marsa Matrouh desert road, right in the heart of Ras Al Hekma, one of the North Coast's most vibrant and investment-attractive areas. The area features an international marina and a ship port, along with easy access to the North Coast's major roads and landmarks.
